I build systems where AI has to earn trust: the model can help, but deterministic controls, reviewable evidence, and human authority remain in charge.

## Selected work

A bidirectional English-Spanish learning world for elementary learners. The private application combines curriculum, practice, assessment, family/teacher oversight, and a browser-local tutor without giving generative AI authority over grading or progression.

### [Vulnerability research](https://github.com/jacobbabula/vulnerability-research)

Responsible, scope-first security research with synthetic proofs, negative controls, evidence boundaries, and coordinated disclosure. Vendor-specific details are published only after clearance; active confidential work—including Proton research—is intentionally absent.

`Application security` `Source review` `Threat modeling` `Responsible disclosure`

## How I work

- **Evidence before claims.** Local reproduction, passing tests, browser acceptance, and production readiness are different proof levels.
- **AI without surrendered authority.** Models advise; deterministic systems grade, authorize, publish, and change state.
- **Safe research by default.** Synthetic data, negative controls, exact version pins, minimal-impact proofs, and coordinated disclosure.
- **Honest limits.** An experimental result is labeled experimental. A duplicate or unverified finding is not presented as an original vulnerability.
